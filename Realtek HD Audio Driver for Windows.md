## 🌟 About

**Realtek HD Audio Driver for Windows** is the official-style audio driver package for **Realtek High Definition Audio codecs** found on most desktop and laptop motherboards. It restores full functionality of your onboard audio — including advanced features missing from the generic Windows drivers.

If your sound is crackling, missing, or you've lost access to the Realtek Audio Console, this is the fix.

> 💡 **Perfect for:** Anyone with onboard Realtek audio (ALC887, ALC892, ALC1200, ALC1220, etc.) on Windows 10 or 11.
